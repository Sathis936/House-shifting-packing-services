7 Proven Ways to Cut Down Shifting Costs on Interstate Relocations

Vikram Sethi (Logistics Economist) • Aug 10, 2026 • 6 min read

Moving across state lines can easily run up substantial bills if not strategized properly. However, over 80% of shifting expenses are determined by volumetric freight space and scheduling calendar demand. Here is how you can slash up to 35% of total moving costs without sacrificing item safety.

1 Book Mid-Week & Mid-Month Slots

Nearly 68% of people move during month-ends and weekend holidays. Logistics companies operate at surge capacity during these windows. Booking between Tuesday and Thursday during the 10th to 20th of the month regularly yields a 15% to 20% discount.

2 Opt for Shared Container Freight (Part Load / LTL)

If your move doesn't require an entire 24ft or 32ft container truck exclusively, opt for Shared Container Freight (LTL). Your belongings are secured in separated steel mesh partitioned bays within the same express container heading along the same highway corridor, saving up to ₹8,000 on toll and fuel surcharges.

3 Aggressively Declutter Prior to On-Site Survey

Moving quotes are calculated by cubic volume (CFT) and total weight. Heavy old wooden desks or broken exercise bikes that cost more to transport than their resale value should be sold or donated before requesting an inventory estimate.
