10 Common Packing Mistakes That Damage Furniture During Long Moves

Elena Rostova (VP of Safety) • Aug 18, 2026 • 6 min read

Relocating your household across cities is a major milestone, but improper packing is the #1 culprit behind broken heirloom dining tables, scratched leather sofas, and shattered televisions.

1. Stacking Plates Flat Instead of Vertically

Plates are engineered to withstand vertical weight from food, not lateral pressure from highway bumps. Always wrap individual ceramic and china plates in double-bubble sheets and pack them standing on edge in partitioned dish cartons.

2. Leaving Furniture Drawers Loaded with Heavy Items

While it seems convenient to leave clothes or books inside dresser drawers, this puts massive strain on joint dowels and wooden sliders during truck transit, often causing total drawer collapse. Empty all drawers into labeled standard boxes.

3. Skipping Corner Protectors on Glass & Marble Tops

Even the thickest moving blanket won't prevent glass edges from chipping if bumped against truck walls. Hard corrugated foam edge guards must be securely taped prior to shrink-wrapping.

Pro Moving Tip from ShiftPro Movers:

Always use colored electrical tape or barcoded labels on boxes matching designated rooms in your new floor plan. This cuts unloading time by over 45%!
