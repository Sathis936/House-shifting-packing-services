Your Step-by-Step Moving Roadmap

We take care of the heavy lifting, logistics, and fragile packing from start to finish.

STAGE 01
7-14 Days Before Moving

Online Instant Estimate & Virtual Home Survey

Use our cost calculator to select your pickup/drop locations and furniture inventory. Our system calculates fixed transparent pricing and assigns your dedicated Move Coordinator.

STAGE 02
Moving Day Morning

Professional 3-Layer Packing & Floor Protection

Our uniformed crew arrives on time with corrugated cartons, bubble wrap, edge guards, and wardrobe boxes. High-value televisions and glass dining sets receive custom foam padding.

STAGE 03
Transit Hours

GPS-Tracked Safe Highway Transit

Your items travel in sanitized, shock-absorbent container trucks. You receive a live tracking link with continuous GPS updates and direct telephone access to your driver.

STAGE 04
Arrival & Setup

Unloading, Room Placement & Debris Cleanup

We unpack items into your specified bedrooms, assemble bed frames and tables, verify the inventory checklist with you, and remove all packaging waste for recycling.

Relocation Preparation Timeline

Inventory & Declutter
