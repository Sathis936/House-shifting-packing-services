Step-by-Step Moving Day Protocol

1

Pre-Move Home Protection

Our crew lays heavy-duty floor runners on wooden/tile flooring and installs padded door jamb protectors to prevent scuffs.

2

Color-Coded Room-by-Room Packing

Boxes are tagged with color labels (Kitchen: Blue, Master Bedroom: Green, Living: Amber) and barcoded for seamless room placement at destination.

3

Air-Suspension Vehicle Transport

Furniture items are strapped securely in enclosed container vans. Real-time GPS tracking link sent directly to your phone.

4

Unpacking & Debris Removal

We reassemble beds and tables, place items in respective rooms, and take all cardboard and tape debris for eco-recycling.
