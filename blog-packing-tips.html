How to Pack Fragile Kitchen Glassware & China Without Breakage

Raj Malhotra (Packing Specialist) • Aug 14, 2026 • 5 min read

Kitchen items, particularly fine china, wine stems, crystal tumblers, and porcelain cookware, account for over 72% of transit damage claims in DIY relocations. The good news? With professional packing geometry and cell partitions, you can achieve zero breakages.

1 The Vertical Stacking Rule for Ceramic Plates

Never stack dinner plates flat like you do in a kitchen cabinet. In transit, road vibrations transfer downward pressure directly through the center of flat plates, creating micro-fractures.

Pro Mover Tip:

Wrap each plate in 2 sheets of blank newsprint, bundle 4 plates together with tape, and stand them vertically on their rim edges inside a heavy-duty dish pack carton lined with crumpled paper padding.

2 Cell Partition Inserts for Stemware & Glasses

Wine glasses and delicate champagne flutes should never touch each other directly. Always use corrugated cellular dividers inside medium cartons.

  • Stuff the interior globe of each wine glass with soft tissue paper to absorb shock.
  • Wrap the delicate stem in 3 layers of 5mm bubble wrap before wrapping the body.
  • Place glasses upright in individual cell pockets, never upside-down on the rim.

3 The "Zero-Rattle" Shake Test

Before sealing any kitchen carton, gently close the flaps and give the box a gentle side-to-side shake. If you feel any internal movement or hear clinking, open the box and fill the voids with crushed unprinted newsprint or foam peanuts until completely snug.

Essential Kitchen Packing Supplies Checklist

5-Ply Heavy Dish Packs (3.0 cu. ft.)
Corrugated Cell Partition Kits
Ink-Free Clean Newsprint (10 kg bundle)
Heavy-Duty 3-Inch Acrylic Packing Tape
